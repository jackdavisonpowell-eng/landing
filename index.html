The work

Four picked out of everything running. Three came out of the AUTOGOD loop: goals I set, built overnight on my hardware, then read, kept and published by me. FRIDAY I wrote by hand.

How it runs

the loop, every night

There is one pattern under everything on this page. I set a goal. The machine does the rest while I sleep, and in the morning I read what it kept.

01

Goal

I write one line in a note. That is the whole brief.

02

Plan

AUTOGOD breaks it into work it can actually finish in a night.

03

Build

On its own GPUs, with its own model. No cloud API, no bill.

04

Judge

It scores what it made against the goal. Then I read it, and I make the call.

05

Keep or kill

Survivors land on the showcase. The rest goes in the log.

Zero idle GPU hours. If a card is on, it is working.

12

Three cards, and the BIOS that would not allow it

Fitting a third compute card was an address-space problem, not a slot problem.

  • Stock firmware’s Above 4G Decoding toggle is broken on that board
  • Flashed a modded BIOS raising high MMIO from 256 GB to 1 TB
  • FreeDOS stick and a patched flasher, since the stock tool rejects modified images
  • Above 4G on, PCIe forced to Gen 3, legacy video off, one cleared CMOS
  • All three run at x8 — the CPU has only 28 lanes to give
