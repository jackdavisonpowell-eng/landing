What is this?

A hub for everything I build. Two servers in Atlanta run my own language models on repurposed data-center GPUs, and every AI and autonomous project that comes off them lands here — what it is, what it runs on, and the part that was actually hard.

AUTOGOD is the agent at the center of it. I give it a goal and it works the stack on its own: picking what to try, building it, judging what it built, keeping what survives and dropping what does not. Most of that happens overnight while I sleep. The showcase is what it kept. The blog is what broke.
