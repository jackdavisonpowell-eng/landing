The machine

thebeast
thebeast — V100 32GB + 2× P100 16GB

It started as one Tesla P100 running a 14B model. Then a second card. Then a 32GB V100 next to them. Today it's 64GB of VRAM on a 2014 workstation board, and it runs a 27B model at Q4 without ever calling out to anyone's API.

FRIDAY, the voice assistant, lives on the V100. AUTOGOD, the worker, gets the pair of P100s. When one of them is idle the other takes the cards — the whole layout is swapped from a panel on the wall.
